# Risk-control matrix

Ratings and tests address indicators for auditor investigation.

| Risk | Expected control | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | C-01: Officers aggregate estimated requirements across the full potential contract term and choose the applicable competition and approval route before commitment. [rule:CPR-037] [rule:CPR-038] [rule:CPR-039] [rule:CPR-108] | T-01A: For flagged supplier windows, reconcile the constituent payments to the underlying requirement, procurement strategy, aggregated contract-value calculation, quotation or tender route, and approvals. Treat recurring legitimate purchases as a possible explanation. The selected windows prioritize two-payment candidates, then higher window values, to broaden supplier coverage across the repeated-window population. |
| R-01 | C-01: Officers aggregate estimated requirements across the full potential contract term and choose the applicable competition and approval route before commitment. [rule:CPR-037] [rule:CPR-038] [rule:CPR-039] [rule:CPR-108] | T-01B: For payments near a printed threshold, compare full contract value and linked payments with the applicable quoted route; establish whether the payment is part of a larger purchase. |
| R-02 | C-02: As an expected accounts-payable control, staff check invoice identity, supporting evidence and prior payments before release; CPR-025 supplies only the general procurement-compliance context, not this payment-specific requirement. [rule:CPR-025] | T-02: Compare each candidate pair's invoice numbers, source invoices, credit notes, recurring-payment schedules, approval trail and any recovery record; establish whether two distinct valid liabilities exist. |
| R-03 | C-03: Procurement staff document the applicable route to market and maintain evidence of award, framework use or approved exception, including required publication. [rule:CPR-025] [rule:CPR-032] [rule:CPR-108] [rule:CPR-115] | T-03: Use the sampled payment as an anchor. Obtain each sampled supplier's complete spend ledger for the three-month window, reconcile the supplier total and procurement category, then compare the current council contract register, frameworks, aliases, waivers and portal records before assessing the applicable route and publication. |
| R-04 | C-04: Before spend continues after a recorded term, the service confirms a live replacement or a contractual extension with the required approval. [rule:CPR-116] | T-04: For each flagged supplier, use the sampled payment as an anchor, obtain the complete payment sequence across the spend window, and compare all payment dates with executed contract terms, any replacement contract, extension clause and S151 Officer approval; resolve notice-export gaps before concluding. |
| R-05 | C-05: Contract owners track cumulative spend against award value and obtain the applicable advance approval for variations. [rule:CPR-117] [rule:CPR-118] | T-05: Compare the flagged annualised estimate with actual cumulative contract spend, original award basis, scope, indexation, term and approved variations; establish the applicable threshold and approval route. |
